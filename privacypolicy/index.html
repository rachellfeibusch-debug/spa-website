Privacy Policy

Pause. Think. Thrive.

Last updated: November 2026

Strategic Pause Advisory Ltd ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and store your personal information in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who we are

Strategic Pause Advisory Ltd is a consultancy firm based in the UK. For the purposes of data protection law, we are the "data controller" of the personal data you provide to us.

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, please contact us at: info@spa-ukltd.com

2. What information we collect

We may collect and process the following types of personal data:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Job title
  • Any other information you choose to share with us when you get in touch or book a call

3. How we collect your data

We collect data when you:

  • Email us
  • Book a call through our booking service, Calendly

This website does not use cookies or analytics, and does not track your visit.

4. Why we collect your data

We use your data to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide requested information
  • Arrange and hold the calls you book
  • Carry out basic marketing activities (with consent)

5. Legal bases for processing

We process your data under the following lawful bases:

  • Consent – when you contact us or book a call voluntarily
  • Legitimate interests – for business communication
  • Legal obligation – where we are required to comply with UK law

6. Cookies

This website does not set cookies.

Some pages contain links to videos on YouTube, a podcast on Spotify and our booking page on Calendly. Videos only load from YouTube when you choose to play them. When you play a video or follow one of these links, the provider may set its own cookies, under its own privacy policy.

7. Third-party services

We use the following third-party service providers:

  • Calendly – for booking calls
  • Microsoft services – for communications and file storage
  • GitHub Pages – to host this website (the host may record technical information such as IP addresses in server logs for security purposes)

These providers may process your data outside the UK/EEA, but we ensure appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) are in place.

8. Data transfers outside the UK

Some data may be processed by our service providers in countries outside the UK/EEA. We rely on lawful data transfer mechanisms approved under UK GDPR.

9. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes it was collected for. Our retention policy aligns with statutory or regulatory requirements, and we will update this policy accordingly.

10. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect data from individuals under the age of 18. If we become aware of such data being collected, we will delete it immediately.

11. Your rights

You have the following rights under the UK GDPR:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request erasure of your data
  • Object to processing or request restriction
  • Withdraw your consent at any time (if applicable)
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)

To exercise your rights, contact us at: info@spa-ukltd.com

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
